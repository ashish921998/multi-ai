## Question

Would a Plannotator-inspired three-pane workspace—room context on the left, a read-only shared requirement or latest Pi plan in the center, and discussion with screenshots on the right—serve this product better than the selected Briefing Room layout while remaining lightweight and avoiding markup, redlines, or a separate plan editor?

## Resolution

Use the Plannotator-inspired Variant D structure: room context and participants on the left, a read-only shared requirement/latest Pi plan in the center, and discussion with screenshots on the right. Reuse visual and interaction patterns where useful, but do not copy Plannotator branding, assets, or unrelated review features. If source code is adapted, preserve the applicable MIT/Apache-2.0 notices. V1 excludes markup, redlines, pinpoint annotations, and a separate editable plan canvas.
